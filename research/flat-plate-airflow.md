# Flat Plate Airflow Research Notes

## Research Question

How does airflow behave over a flat surface and what can this teach us about aircraft aerodynamics?

## Background

The flat plate is one of the most common models used in fluid mechanics and aerodynamics research.

Studying airflow over a flat plate helps engineers understand:

* Boundary layers
* Flow separation
* Turbulence
* Drag forces

before moving to more complex aircraft geometries.

## Boundary Layer Development

As air moves over a surface, friction causes the airflow nearest the surface to slow down.

This region is called the boundary layer.

### Laminar Boundary Layer

Characteristics:

* Smooth airflow
* Predictable motion
* Lower drag

### Turbulent Boundary Layer

Characteristics:

* Chaotic motion
* Increased mixing
* Higher drag

## Flow Separation

Flow separation occurs when airflow detaches from a surface.

Effects:

* Increased drag
* Turbulence formation
* Reduced aerodynamic efficiency

## Application to Project

Before testing paper airplanes, a flat plate can be placed inside the wind tunnel to visualize:

* Airflow attachment
* Boundary layer formation
* Turbulence development

This provides a baseline experiment for comparison.
