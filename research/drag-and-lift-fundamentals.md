# Drag and Lift Fundamentals Research Notes

## Research Question

What aerodynamic forces affect aircraft and paper airplane flight?

## The Four Forces of Flight

All flying vehicles experience four primary forces:

### Lift

Lift is the aerodynamic force that acts perpendicular to the direction of airflow.

Lift opposes gravity and helps keep an aircraft airborne.

### Weight

Weight is the force caused by gravity acting on the aircraft.

Weight acts downward toward Earth.

### Thrust

Thrust is the force that moves an aircraft forward.

For paper airplanes, thrust comes from the initial throw.

### Drag

Drag is the aerodynamic force that opposes forward motion through the air.

Drag acts opposite the direction of travel.

## Lift Generation

Lift is generated when airflow interacts with a wing.

Factors affecting lift include:

* Wing shape
* Angle of attack
* Airspeed
* Wing area
* Air density

## Pressure Differences

According to Bernoulli's Principle, pressure tends to decrease as fluid velocity increases.

Pressure differences around a wing contribute to lift generation.

Modern aerodynamic analysis also incorporates Newton's Laws, emphasizing downward deflection of airflow.

## Types of Drag

### Parasite Drag

Drag that does not contribute to lift production.

Includes:

* Form drag
* Skin-friction drag
* Interference drag

### Induced Drag

Drag associated with lift generation.

Typically increases at lower flight speeds and higher lift conditions.

## Relevance to Paper Airplanes

Paper airplanes provide a simple platform for observing aerodynamic forces.

Different designs influence:

* Lift production
* Drag generation
* Flight stability
* Glide performance

## Application to Wind Tunnel Project

The project will compare airplane geometries to investigate:

* Airflow patterns
* Relative drag characteristics
* Stability behavior
