# Smoke Flow Visualization Research Notes

## Research Question

How can smoke be used to visualize airflow in a wind tunnel?

## Background

Because air is invisible, engineers use tracer particles such as smoke to reveal airflow patterns.

Smoke visualization is commonly used in aerospace engineering laboratories and educational wind tunnels.

## Smoke Tracers

Purpose:

* Make airflow visible
* Identify streamline behavior
* Observe turbulence

## Flow Visualization Techniques

### Incense Smoke

Advantages:

* Low cost
* Easy to obtain
* Continuous smoke production

Disadvantages:

* Lower density
* Sensitive to room airflow

### Fog Machines

Advantages:

* Dense flow visualization
* Easy photography

Disadvantages:

* Higher cost

### Laser Sheet Visualization

Method:
A laser sheet illuminates smoke particles.

Advantages:

* Produces detailed airflow images

Disadvantages:

* More complex setup
* Safety considerations

## Expected Observations

### Laminar Flow

Characteristics:

* Smooth streamlines
* Minimal mixing

### Turbulent Flow

Characteristics:

* Chaotic motion
* Rapid mixing

### Flow Separation

Characteristics:

* Streamlines detach from surfaces
* Wake formation occurs

## Application to Project

Smoke visualization will allow comparison of airflow behavior around different paper airplane designs.

## Poster-Ready Citation

"Smoke visualization provides a simple method for observing airflow attachment, turbulence, and wake formation around aerodynamic models."
