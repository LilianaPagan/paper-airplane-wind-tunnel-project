# FoldNFly Airplane Design Research Notes

## Research Question

Which paper airplane designs should be selected for wind tunnel testing and aerodynamic comparison?

## Background

Fold'NFly provides a large collection of paper airplane designs categorized by flight characteristics such as distance, stability, speed, and gliding performance.

Project testing will focus on comparing multiple airplane geometries to observe airflow patterns and aerodynamic behavior.

## Candidate Airplanes

### The Stable

Characteristics:

* Designed for stable flight
* Wide wing geometry
* Beginner friendly

Expected Wind Tunnel Observations:

* Smooth airflow attachment
* Reduced turbulence
* Stable wake region

---

### Bulldog Dart

Characteristics:

* Compact design
* Fast flight
* Narrow wing profile

Expected Wind Tunnel Observations:

* Lower frontal area
* Reduced drag
* Faster airflow over surfaces

---

### Hammer

Characteristics:

* Long-distance design
* Balanced geometry

Expected Wind Tunnel Observations:

* Moderate drag
* Good stability characteristics

---

### Sea Gull

Characteristics:

* Large wing area
* High lift design

Expected Wind Tunnel Observations:

* Increased lift effects
* Larger airflow interaction region

---

### Nakamura Lock

Characteristics:

* Popular competition design
* Strong structural integrity

Expected Wind Tunnel Observations:

* Stable airflow patterns
* Consistent aerodynamic performance

## Future Experiment Goals

Compare:

* Wing size
* Wing shape
* Stability
* Turbulence generation
* Airflow attachment
