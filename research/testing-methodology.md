# Testing Methodology Research Notes

## Research Question

What experimental procedures should be used to ensure consistent and meaningful wind tunnel observations?

---

## Background

Engineering experiments require a structured methodology to ensure results are reliable and repeatable.

Even simple educational wind tunnel projects should follow basic experimental principles.

---

## Experimental Controls

A control is a condition that remains constant throughout testing.

Examples:

* Same fan speed
* Same tunnel configuration
* Same smoke source
* Same observation distance
* Same lighting conditions

Maintaining controls improves comparison between airplane designs.

---

## Repeatability

Repeatability refers to obtaining similar results when an experiment is performed multiple times under identical conditions.

Benefits:

* Increased reliability
* Improved confidence in observations
* Better engineering practice

---

## Observation Sheets

Observation sheets should record:

* Date
* Airplane design
* Fan setting
* Airflow observations
* Turbulence observations
* Stability observations

Observation sheets improve organization and consistency.

---

## Qualitative Observations

Qualitative observations describe what is seen.

Examples:

* Smooth airflow
* Large turbulent wake
* Stable airflow attachment
* Flow separation near wing edge

No numerical measurement is required.

---

## Quantitative Measurements

Quantitative measurements involve numerical values.

Possible measurements include:

* Airflow velocity
* Flight distance
* Flight duration
* Tunnel dimensions

These measurements provide objective data.

---

## Data Collection Procedures

Recommended procedure:

1. Install airplane model.
2. Set fan speed.
3. Introduce smoke flow.
4. Observe airflow behavior.
5. Record observations.
6. Photograph results.
7. Repeat for additional designs.

---

## Application to Project

A consistent testing methodology will allow meaningful comparison of:

* Dart airplanes
* Glider airplanes
* Competition designs
* Experimental configurations

---

## Poster-Ready Citation

"Maintaining consistent experimental controls improves repeatability and enables meaningful comparison between aerodynamic designs."
