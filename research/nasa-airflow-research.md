# NASA Airflow Research Notes

## Main Topics

* Lift
* Drag
* Airflow visualization
* Wind tunnels
* Turbulence

## Key Findings

NASA uses smoke visualization in wind tunnels to observe airflow behavior around aerodynamic bodies. Streamline patterns help engineers identify turbulence, drag regions, and flow separation.

## Relevance to Project

This information supports the use of smoke visualization techniques in the tabletop acrylic wind tunnel for observing airflow around paper airplane designs.
