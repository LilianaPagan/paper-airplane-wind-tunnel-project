# Undergraduate Wind Tunnel Projects Research Notes

## Research Question

How have undergraduate engineering programs used wind tunnels for educational research and aerospace outreach?

---

## Background

Wind tunnel projects are common in undergraduate engineering education because they provide hands-on experience with aerodynamic principles, experimental methods, and engineering design.

Many student projects focus on visualization rather than advanced computational analysis.

---

## Similar University Projects

Common project themes include:

* Airfoil testing
* Model aircraft testing
* Flow visualization
* Drag reduction studies
* Low-cost wind tunnel construction

These projects often emphasize learning and experimentation rather than producing publishable research.

---

## Educational Wind Tunnel Designs

Typical undergraduate wind tunnels include:

* Acrylic construction
* Box fan airflow systems
* Honeycomb flow straighteners
* Transparent test sections

Advantages:

* Affordable
* Easy to build
* Effective for demonstrations

---

## Student Aerospace Outreach

Wind tunnels are frequently used during:

* Engineering open houses
* STEM fairs
* Recruitment events
* Aerospace demonstrations

Benefits include:

* Increased student engagement
* Visual learning
* Public interest in engineering

---

## Lessons Learned from Previous Teams

Common recommendations include:

### Keep the Design Simple

Complex systems often delay construction and testing.

### Focus on Repeatability

Consistent airflow is more important than maximum airflow speed.

### Prioritize Visualization

Clear smoke flow patterns improve educational value.

### Document Everything

Photos, sketches, and notes improve project quality and poster preparation.

---

## Application to Project

This project follows many of the same goals as undergraduate aerospace engineering laboratories:

* Build a low-cost wind tunnel
* Study airflow behavior
* Support STEM outreach
* Engage students in experimental aerodynamics
