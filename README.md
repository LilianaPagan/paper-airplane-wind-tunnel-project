# 2026 Wind Tunnel and Paper Airplane Aerodynamics Research Project

## Overview

This undergraduate aerospace engineering project focuses on designing and constructing a low-cost acrylic wind tunnel to study airflow around paper airplane designs using smoke visualization techniques.

The project explores:

* Lift
* Drag
* Airflow visualization
* Stability
* Turbulence
* Aerodynamic efficiency

## Objectives

* Build a tabletop acrylic wind tunnel
* Study airflow around paper airplanes
* Compare aerodynamic designs
* Visualize turbulence and airflow patterns
* Encourage student collaboration in STEM

## Research Areas

* Wind tunnel airflow visualization
* Paper airplane aerodynamics
* Airflow calculations
* Experimental aerodynamics
* Aerospace engineering education
