# Paper Airplane Competition Research Notes

## Research Question

How can paper airplane competitions be used to encourage STEM engagement while demonstrating aerodynamic principles?

---

## Background

Paper airplane competitions have been used by schools, museums, engineering organizations, and aerospace outreach programs to teach flight mechanics, design optimization, and experimental testing.

Because paper airplanes are inexpensive and easy to construct, they provide an accessible introduction to aerospace engineering concepts.

---

## Guinness Distance Records

Competitive paper airplane design often focuses on maximizing flight distance.

Distance-focused airplanes generally feature:

* Narrow wing configurations
* High structural rigidity
* Aerodynamically efficient shapes
* Stable center of gravity

Engineering Concepts Demonstrated:

* Drag reduction
* Flight stability
* Energy conservation
* Launch optimization

---

## Flight Duration Competitions

Flight duration competitions focus on maximizing time aloft.

Successful designs typically have:

* Large wing area
* Increased lift generation
* Slower descent rates
* Glider-style configurations

Engineering Concepts Demonstrated:

* Lift generation
* Wing loading
* Glide ratio
* Stability

---

## Accuracy Competitions

Accuracy competitions require participants to hit a target.

Success depends on:

* Consistent airplane construction
* Flight stability
* Repeatable launch techniques

Engineering Concepts Demonstrated:

* Repeatability
* Precision engineering
* Experimental consistency

---

## Campus STEM Engagement

A paper airplane challenge can help:

* Introduce students to aerospace engineering
* Promote hands-on learning
* Encourage teamwork
* Increase interest in STEM programs
* Provide outreach opportunities for prospective students

Potential Participants:

* Engineering students
* STEM clubs
* Faculty
* Local high school students

## Application to Wind Tunnel Project

Competition airplane designs may be tested in the wind tunnel before competition day.

This provides students with an opportunity to:

* Observe airflow behavior
* Compare designs
* Apply aerodynamic principles
* Improve performance through experimentation
