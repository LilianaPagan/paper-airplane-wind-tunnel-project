# Wind Tunnel Design Basics Research Notes

## Research Question

What are the essential components of a wind tunnel and how can they be adapted for a low-cost educational project?

## Background

Wind tunnels are experimental tools used to study airflow around objects. They allow engineers to observe aerodynamic phenomena in a controlled environment.

Modern wind tunnels vary in size from small educational models to large facilities used by NASA and aircraft manufacturers.

## Major Wind Tunnel Sections

### Settling Chamber

Purpose:

* Reduce turbulence
* Smooth incoming airflow

Common Components:

* Screens
* Honeycomb flow straighteners

### Contraction Section

Purpose:

* Accelerate airflow
* Improve airflow uniformity

Engineering Principle:
As cross-sectional area decreases, airflow velocity increases.

### Test Section

Purpose:

* Area where models are placed
* Primary observation region

Requirements:

* Transparent walls
* Uniform airflow
* Adequate lighting

### Diffuser Section

Purpose:

* Slow airflow before exiting
* Reduce energy losses

### Fan Section

Purpose:

* Generate airflow through the tunnel

Common Educational Choices:

* Box fans
* Inline duct fans

## Application to Project

The proposed wind tunnel will include:

* Acrylic test section
* Honeycomb airflow straightener
* Box fan drive system
* Smoke visualization capability

## Poster-Ready Citation

"Educational wind tunnels use controlled airflow to investigate aerodynamic behavior and visualize flow patterns around test objects."
